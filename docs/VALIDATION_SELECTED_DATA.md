# Validation selected comparison data dictionary

All36 selection-validation models and36 final-test models have stable run_id; pair_id joins the two final arms. dataset is ml1m or amazon_kindle; copy identifies one of three fixed splits. candidate is C01–C06. label 2026102701 belongs only to selection; final uses 2026102702–2026102704. selection scores are not test scores. Arms are private and matched_no_training_noise.

- candidate_results.csv retains36 per-copy validation ndcg/recall and frozen parameters; candidate_selection_summary.csv contains12 equal-copy candidate means and tie sets.
- final_run_manifest.csv contains36 locked final identities and seven-coordinate entropy vectors. Vector coordinates are namespace,dataset code,copy,stage,label,candidate integer,stream. Streams1/2/3 are preprocessing/initialization/training noise. Model IDs do not change when a local path changes.
- model_identity.csv has72 source model/factor/ledger content digests, actual preprocessing/init hashes, terminal diagnostics and timing. Digests identify excluded factors; they do not redistribute those factors or their interactions. ledger_events.csv has504 rows keyed by event_id=run_id:eNN. sensitivity,noise_sd,gaussian_multiplier and repetitions are actual event quantities; sweep identifies training iteration. Count events lack a sweep. protected is a channel-level indicator, not a claim that M's complete output is DP. nominal_private_noise_sd remains recorded for M, but its actual training noise_sd is0.
- ledger_summary.csv records the actual-event RDP parameterization alpha*rho, conversion, orders and delta. M has no complete epsilon/optimal_order. The count subtotal is not complete-output privacy.
- metrics_by_run.csv has36 final test absolute NDCG20 and Recall20. paired_results.csv has18 M/P pairs; P_minus_M is private minus matched. loss_percent=100*(M-P)/M, undefined if M=0. summary_by_copy.csv uses three seeds equally; summary_by_task.csv weights copies equally and seeds equally within copy. descriptive_pair_loss_sd is sample dispersion, not a confidence interval.
- selected_parameters.json and selection_lock.json preserve all candidate evidence, the selected configuration per task, final expansion and content bindings for the portable records. This public normalization is derived from the original execution lock and includes its digest; it is not an externally immutable preregistration or a claim that the source lock was rewritten.
- independent_review.json states the independent metric/preprocessing/accounting methods and the later report-assembly boundary. audit_* tables retain the numerical checks. Diagnostics and source reviews cannot prove unmonitored historical test access or replay unsaved intermediate PSD matrices.

No candidate,seed,unfavorable metric or undefined pair is dropped. There are no raw interactions, model binaries, user/item factors, credentials, workstation paths, reviewer communications or manuscript documents in this update. Data split paths are relative to a user-supplied data root; content hashes identify legal benchmark inputs. Records are exploratory research releases, not a single-model-epsilon public archive guarantee.

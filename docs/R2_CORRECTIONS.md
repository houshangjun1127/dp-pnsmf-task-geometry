# R2 corrections and evidence boundaries

The new two-profile Private ALS comparison supersedes the legacy DPALS-style stress test as the current baseline evidence. Historical results/tags are retained. The old same-budget claim lacked full independent Gram/RHS/shared-Gram accounting; do not reuse it as a fair-comparison premise. A larger sufficient upper bound is not an observed privacy leakage or a lower bound. Historical commands now issue a legacy warning.

The privacy interpretation is trusted central DP: the curator can see the unnoised aggregate and is outside the protected adversary view. Secure aggregation was simulated, not implemented as a cryptographic protection against that curator. Fixed public slots, explicit present/null adjacency, persistent-state reasoning and a single secret-random shared transcript are essential; see R2_PRIVACY_MODEL.md. Public seeds, user factors, diagnostic/holdout metrics, nonprivate controls and the joint research archive do not inherit one epsilon.

Same-batch SNR: the multiseed batch has 30 pairs per dataset, ten labels shared across three fixed copies. All90 private run time-medians are below1. The dataset means are about 0.094925 (MovieLens), 0.013215 (Amazon), 0.017862 (Netflix); take exact values from snr_paired_audit.csv. Their paired ranking losses are from this same batch, not a different frozen three-arm batch. Low query SNR alone does not distinguish severe task ranking loss. Table 8 retains its conditional shared-seed intervals; they are not carried into the three-label ALS comparison.

Three-arm decomposition uses the frozen batch with F (full nonprivate), M (sampling/clipping matched), P (private). Decompose absolute differences: F-P=(F-M)+(M-P). Percentages use their declared denominators; averaging pair percentages differs from the ratio of means. For Amazon the equal-copy mean absolute gaps are approximately0.044800633 and0.009983615. The corresponding mean F-to-M percentage over F is77.977898%, and M-to-P over M is78.902110%; these percentages cannot be added. These values do not replace the multiseed78.330127% result.

Convergence: 200 rounds fixes the privacy budget and training horizon. Amazon matched controls still improve between700 and800 rounds. These endpoints are not an optimization equilibrium or a causal learning-rate effect; no800-round private training was added.

Theory verification: for each of the three fixed factor states, the existing code used500 independent Gaussian quadratic-form samples; the1000 sign-paired values are antithetic, not1000 independent full objectives. Only 4 direct full-objective identity evaluations were performed per dataset. The conditional quadratic identity holds for fixed user factors and one isotropic item perturbation; it is not a multiround ranking guarantee. theory_scope_audit.csv records arithmetic/scope checks and explicitly marks the Monte Carlo reexecution as not performed in that audit. This package does not repeat those Monte Carlo experiments.

Execution note: two initial lock/file IO ordering failures were retained; after checking the existing full lock, a single startup recovery preceded synthetic checks and all real training. All72 model attempts were1, with no training retry or seed replacement. Independent review initially misrequired DP-only metadata on matched non-DP output; the check scope was corrected with both versions retained. These incidents did not change models. Local timestamps and hashes support consistency, not immutable external preregistration. Internal logs are excluded.

These are result interpretation corrections and packaging of already completed work. No new benchmark training, tuning, manuscript editing or publication occurred during preparation. Numerical/integrity checks do not establish competitive optimality or the causal origin of low utility.
